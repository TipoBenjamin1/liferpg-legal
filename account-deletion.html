Account Deletion

LifeRPG is operated by Polejaev Vladislav, an individual based in the Republic of Moldova. This page explains account deletion, what it removes and what can remain. Account deletion is permanent. Signing out, disabling sync or uninstalling the app does not delete the server account.

Delete from the app

  1. Open LifeRPG and go to Community (“Сообщество”). Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Select “Delete account and server data” (“Удалить аккаунт и серверные данные”). You do not need to create a public player profile or accept community rules just to delete an account you can sign in to.
  3. Read the consequences and confirm. Keep the operation available until the app confirms completion.

If you cannot sign in, use password recovery or the external request route below. A successful sign-in is required for the in-app request; an email address alone does not authorise deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email a deletion request to liferpg.official.help@gmail.com with the subject “LifeRPG account deletion”, preferably from the email address registered to your account. State that you want your LifeRPG account and its associated data deleted. You do not need to reinstall the app to use this route.

If you no longer control the registered email, explain the access problem. Support will need to establish account ownership before authorising deletion and may be unable to proceed until it can be verified. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, session tokens or identity documents in the initial request. We will explain any necessary next step through the support channel.

This is a request for actual account deletion, not account deactivation. A sent email is not a completion confirmation. The support channel will confirm the outcome after verification and processing. We do not promise an instant response or round-the-clock support.

What is removed when deletion succeeds

The successful server transaction removes your Supabase Auth login account and linked active account records, including:

  • Public player profile, account settings and community-rule acceptance.
  • Friendships, blocks, guild membership and related invitations.
  • Private synced tasks and progress, cloud RPG XP and LifeCoins, inventory, reward and purchase records.
  • Cloud Academy attempts, answers, explanations, entitlements and linked ranked records.
  • Account-linked notification and push records, where present.
  • Reports you submitted and reports targeting the profile being deleted.

You lose access to this cloud progress, social identity and its rewards. Deletion does not remove other users' accounts or private progress. If you lead a guild, leadership transfers to the senior officer or another member. A guild with no remaining members is removed. Shared guild names, descriptions and emblems may remain for other members; contact liferpg.official.help@gmail.com if they contain your personal information.

Local progress is a separate choice

Confirmed in-app deletion clears the account session, cloud caches, pending sync, ranked cache and temporary export copy. It deliberately preserves local tasks, XP, LifeCoins, skills, achievements, Academy history, routes, steps and inventory. That local database is blocked from linking its retained progress to another cloud account.

To erase local history too, first resolve any pending account deletion and finish or remove an active LifeTrack walk. Sign out if still signed in, then use “More → Delete all local data” (“Ещё → Удалить все локальные данные”) and confirm. Export first only if you want a separate copy; exported JSON is not a guarantee of full restoration. A web or email request cannot remotely erase information on an offline device. Remove saved exports and copies shared with other services separately. Original Health Connect records created by other apps are not deleted by LifeRPG.

What can remain and for how long

  • A minimal deletion receipt keeps a random operation ID, a SHA-256 binding and completion time to confirm an interrupted request. It has no email, display name, raw account ID or game content. It remains a technical pseudonymous record. No automatic expiry or cleanup is currently configured; no fixed retention deadline has been established.
  • Existing encrypted disaster-recovery snapshots are separate copies and are not rewritten by account deletion. No automatic snapshot expiry has been established. Later recovery must reconcile deletion requests before bringing accounts back online.
  • Supabase Auth audit/infrastructure logs and Brevo delivery/event records, and any saved email previews, are not all removed by the database transaction. Their account-specific automatic expiry periods have not been confirmed. Brevo's documented default can retain logs without a time limit. This page does not promise a 24-month or shorter universal expiry.
  • Support and moderation emails remain in the support mailbox until separately removed; no fixed automatic retention period has been adopted. Request deletion of related correspondence when you contact support.
  • Local history and exported copies remain under the separate controls above. A shared guild remains until changed or removed through its controls or moderation. Ask support to remove personal information from shared guild text.

These limited residuals do not preserve your active cloud account or ordinary game progress. Any actual legal preservation requirement must be explained for the particular request. See the Privacy Policy for the provider and backup details.

The active account records listed above are removed when the transaction commits. A support request first needs ownership verification and processing. No unsupported processing or purge deadline is promised.

Interrupted request or missing confirmation

If the connection fails, the request may already have completed. Reopen the app and allow it to check the saved operation; if it offers a retry, retry the existing operation. Do not assume an error means the account still exists, or that it proves deletion succeeded. Confirmation uses a minimal receipt and can work after the original login session has gone.

If the result remains uncertain or deletion fails, contact liferpg.official.help@gmail.com. Include the approximate request time, app version and error text with sensitive information removed. Do not post operation identifiers or credentials publicly. Support does not need your password.
