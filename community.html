Scope and acceptance

LifeRPG is operated by Polejaev Vladislav, an individual based in the Republic of Moldova. The operator is responsible for community moderation; the developer handles technical support. The moderation and appeal contact is liferpg.official.help@gmail.com.

Community rules version 2026-11-15 must be explicitly accepted before publishing or changing protected profile or guild content. The server records the accepted version and time. These rules apply to usernames, display names, symbolic avatars, selected appearance, shared skill and achievement names, and guild names, descriptions and emblems.

What is not allowed

  • Threats, bullying, harassment, stalking, discrimination, hate speech or encouragement of violence.
  • Sexual or pornographic content, child sexual abuse or exploitation, grooming, or any sexualisation of minors.
  • Fraud, impersonation, spam or advertising through profile and guild fields.
  • Publishing another person's personal information, account credentials or other secrets.
  • Copyright infringement, illegal material or instructions to commit illegal acts.
  • Manipulation of rewards, activity validation, rankings or reports, including deliberately false or abusive reports.

Do not use public fields to share private tasks, email addresses, route coordinates or information that could expose you or another person to harm.

Report a profile or guild

Open the player profile and choose “Report profile” (“Пожаловаться на профиль”), or choose “Report guild” (“Пожаловаться на гильдию”) for guild content. Select a reason and add a short explanation if helpful. A successful submission returns a report identifier. Your reports and their statuses are available in the app's reports area.

An existing unresolved report about the same target can be reused, and rate limits apply. Do not submit repeated reports to pressure another player. Do not include passwords, codes or unnecessary sensitive information. If the in-app route is unavailable, email liferpg.official.help@gmail.com.

Block another player

Use “Block” in the player's card to stop friend requests and invitations between you and that player and limit profile interactions. You can also open and block the profile of a member of your guild. Blocking does not delete a shared guild or remove every trace of shared content.

How reports are handled

An authorised moderator can review reports, remove inappropriate public text or appearance, and restrict further publication. Report statuses include received, under review, resolved and rejected. Ordinary users cannot browse other users' private report queues.

Moderation is not an emergency service. No round-the-clock coverage or fixed decision deadline is promised. The operator must assess reports and any legal duties that apply to the particular case. A report is not a guarantee that a requested restriction will be imposed.

Child safety and urgent concerns

LifeRPG is intended for people aged 13 and older, subject to stricter local requirements. Child sexual abuse and exploitation, grooming and related content are prohibited.

Report suspected content through the app or liferpg.official.help@gmail.com. Include the profile or guild identifier and a short description. Do not download, reproduce or send illegal images to support. If someone is in immediate danger, contact the relevant local emergency service; do not wait for an in-app moderation response.

The operator is the contact for child-safety concerns. This page does not claim that an automated child-safety detection system, a specialised reporting integration or a staffed emergency escalation service exists.

Ask for a decision to be reviewed

Email liferpg.official.help@gmail.com with the relevant report, profile or guild identifier, the approximate date and why you believe a decision should be reconsidered. Keep private information out of public posts. Support will not ask for your password or authentication codes.

Read the Privacy Policy for report data and retention, the Terms of Service for use of the app, and Account Deletion to remove your account.
