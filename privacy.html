Who is responsible for your data

LifeRPG is operated by Polejaev Vladislav, an individual based in the Republic of Moldova, who is the data controller for the app. User support is provided by the developer on behalf of the operator. For privacy questions or requests, contact liferpg.official.help@gmail.com. For general support, contact liferpg.official.help@gmail.com.

This policy explains how LifeRPG handles information when you use its productivity, RPG progress, learning, social and activity features. Many features work locally without an account. Creating an account, publishing a social profile, personal cloud sync, linking RPG progress, location access and step access involve separate choices.

Information stored on your device

LifeRPG stores your character, name and avatar, tasks and descriptions, completions, skills, XP, LifeCoins, achievements, rest days, inventory, Academy answers and explanations, results and completion times in a local database. Activity features also store step summaries, walks and GPS routes when you use them.

Local information is not automatically uploaded merely because you install the app or create an account. The database is not represented as encrypted storage. Protect access to your device and exported files.

Accounts and cloud progress

When you register or sign in, your email address and password are sent over HTTPS to Supabase Auth. LifeRPG uses an account ID to associate your online records. Your password is not stored in the local game database; mobile session credentials use the operating system's protected credential storage. Your email address is not included in your public player profile.

If you enable personal sync, LifeRPG sends the supported character details, skills, task titles and descriptions, completions, XP history, achievements and rest days to your private cloud archive. Completed Academy attempts include question IDs, answers, written explanations, results and timing. Unfinished ordinary lessons are not uploaded by this archive. Do not put passwords, sensitive documents or other people's personal information into tasks or answers.

Online ranked challenges send the answers and results needed for server validation. Cloud RPG records include verified progress, LifeCoins, reward and purchase records, inventory and selected equipment. Local guest balances and cloud balances are separate; cloud sync is not a complete backup of every item in the local database.

Social features, reports and moderation

Other players can see the account ID, username, display name, symbolic avatar, selected appearance, guild membership and relevant competitive results during social interactions. Discoverability by name and sharing XP, skills and achievements have separate controls. An exact player ID can still identify a profile when name search is disabled. Guild names, descriptions and emblems are shared content.

We store friend relationships, blocks, invitations, guild roles and relevant social notifications to operate these features. Publishing requires acceptance of the community rules. We record the rules version and acceptance time.

Reports contain the reporter and target IDs, reason, optional explanation, status and timestamps. An authorised moderator can review a report, remove inappropriate public fields and restrict further publication. Other players cannot read your private report queue through the app. Use liferpg.official.help@gmail.com for moderation questions or to ask for a decision to be reviewed. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information in a report.

Steps, activity and location

On supported Android devices, LifeRPG reads steps from Health Connect with your permission, including up to 30 available days. It does not write steps to Health Connect or read other Health Connect record types. On iOS, step features use Core Motion, including up to seven available days; they do not use HealthKit. Motion sensors can also help distinguish walking from other movement during a walk.

Steps and walking information support daily goals, history, challenges and progress rewards. They are initially kept locally. If you separately link RPG progress to your account, confirmed daily steps, walking distances, dates and times, completed challenges and feature availability are sent to Supabase to validate progress and rewards. Original Health Connect records and full GPS routes are not uploaded through this process. LifeRPG does not use this activity data for advertising.

LifeTrack uses precise location during a walk you start. Recording can continue while the screen is locked or the app is in the background. Android uses a visible foreground-service notification; iOS uses its location permission and system indicators. Pausing or finishing a walk stops recording. This is not continuous tracking outside a walk you start, and uninterrupted recording after force-closing the app is not guaranteed.

Full routes remain in the local database and are not part of Supabase sync. If you open the online map, the app loads MapLibre resources from unpkg.com and map resources from tiles.openfreemap.org. These providers receive your IP address and resource or tile requests, together with ordinary network and browser information. The requested map area can reveal a precise geographic area even though the complete route is not uploaded to Supabase. OpenFreeMap states that its normal access logs do not store IP addresses, error logs may include IP addresses and URLs and are deleted after seven days, and security-incident IP logging can last up to 30 days. It may also use Cloudflare. See the OpenFreeMap privacy policy. UNPKG uses Cloudflare to deliver the map library; no specific LifeRPG-controlled deletion period is established for its CDN logs. See UNPKG and Cloudflare privacy information.

You can revoke location, motion or Health Connect access in the operating system or Health Connect settings. Revoking access stops new permitted reads; it does not erase previously stored history. Deleting LifeRPG data does not delete the original Health Connect records created by other apps. LifeRPG is not a medical device or a service for diagnosis or treatment.

Notifications, exports and technical information

Task reminders are scheduled locally. Task titles may appear on the lock screen, depending on your device settings. Remote push delivery is disabled in the current release configuration. Server-side push infrastructure exists, but its presence does not mean that remote device registration or delivery is enabled. If push is enabled in a later release, the notice must describe its actual token and delivery processing.

The app does not currently integrate its own advertising, analytics or automatic crash-reporting service. Android dependencies include Firebase Messaging/Installations and related SDK components; included components are not, by themselves, proof that all of their services are active. LifeRPG does not request an Expo push token in this release configuration because remote push is not enabled. Included platform libraries can still perform their own service or diagnostic processing; this policy does not claim that dependency presence proves a particular transmission or that every network operation has been independently measured. Network providers receive information such as IP addresses when handling requests; provider-side logs and diagnostics follow the retention information below.

JSON exports can include tasks, learning answers, activity and route coordinates. An export is shared with the destination you choose using the system share menu. LifeRPG clears its temporary export copy after that flow and during confirmed in-app account deletion. Copies you save or send elsewhere remain under your or the recipient's control. An export is not a promise of automatic import or complete restoration.

Why data is used and who receives it

Data is used to provide the features you choose: authentication, private progress sync, server validation of rewards, social interactions, community safety, account deletion and support. LifeRPG does not sell personal data or use Health Connect information for advertising.

Supabase processes account and cloud data. The production database is hosted in eu-west-1 (Ireland). See Supabase's data processing addendum and subprocessor list. These links describe the provider's terms; they do not identify an unconfirmed contract, representative or transfer arrangement for LifeRPG. Supabase sends registration confirmation, repeat confirmation and password-recovery emails through Brevo SMTP. Brevo receives the destination email address, sender details, message subject and HTML/text content, including the one-time authentication code or link generated by Supabase. It processes delivery, failure and engagement events and the network information needed to deliver emails and handle tracked links. LifeRPG does not send game tasks, GPS routes, step history or passwords to Brevo for these messages. These are service emails, not marketing subscriptions.

Brevo's anonymous transactional email tracking is enabled. Opens and clicks can still be counted, and links can pass through Brevo before reaching Supabase and the app. This is not a claim that tracking is switched off, that no network information is processed, or that authentication links remain valid indefinitely. The Brevo tracking explanation describes the effect on recipient-level reports. See also the Brevo privacy policy. Map/CDN providers receive the map requests described above. Support and moderation correspondence is processed using Google Gmail. GitHub Pages hosts this legal website. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security, including for signed-out visitors. These pages add no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, cookies, external fonts or forms. Opening an external provider link or sending email involves the chosen provider. See GitHub's privacy statement and Gmail privacy information. Public content is available to its social audience, and exported information is received by your chosen destination.

Providers or support personnel may process information outside your country. The primary Supabase database region is Ireland; email, support and CDN processing can involve other countries. Contact the operator for the legal grounds and international safeguards applicable to your circumstances. This policy does not claim a particular certification, representative or transfer mechanism that has not been established for LifeRPG. Permission to use a device feature does not, by itself, determine every legal basis for processing.

Storage, account deletion and remaining records

Local history remains until you deliberately remove it. Active cloud account records remain while the account is used, unless you delete particular supported records or delete the account.

Use “Delete account and server data” in Community, or follow the account deletion instructions. When the deletion transaction succeeds, it removes your Auth account and linked active records: profile, settings, friendships, blocks, invitations, personal sync archive, cloud RPG progress and inventory, Academy records, linked rankings, notification records and community acceptance. Reports you submitted and reports targeting your deleted profile are removed. Other users' accounts and private progress are not deleted.

If you lead a guild, leadership transfers to an officer or another member; an empty guild is removed. A guild with remaining members and its shared name or description may remain. Ask the moderator to review personal information in shared content.

A minimal technical deletion receipt remains so that an interrupted request can be confirmed without a surviving login session. It holds a random request ID, a SHA-256 binding of the account and request IDs, and a completion timestamp. It does not contain your email, display name, task content or raw account ID. It is a technical pseudonymous record, not a claim of irreversible anonymisation. There is currently no automatic expiry or scheduled cleanup for these receipts. They remain until removed through an authorised maintenance process; no fixed deletion deadline has been established.

Account deletion does not rewrite existing recovery snapshots or instantly erase every infrastructure log. The following limits are separate from deletion of active game and account records:

  • Encrypted disaster-recovery snapshots exist outside the active database, including a private Google Drive copy. They are protected by a separate key and restricted operator access. No automatic expiry has been established for these snapshots. They may contain records present when the snapshot was made. Deletion requests must be reconciled before any recovery is used to resume the service; an old snapshot must not silently reinstate a deleted account.
  • Supabase documents a one-day retention window for API and database logs on the Free plan used by LifeRPG. This is not a promise that all provider copies, diagnostics or database-held Auth audit records are physically erased after one day. Auth audit records stored in the database are separate; no automatic cleanup period for those records has been confirmed. See Supabase log retention and Auth audit logs.
  • Brevo delivery logs, email-event records and any saved message previews are separate from the Supabase deletion transaction. The account's exact retention and preview settings have not been verified. Brevo documents that logs can be kept without a time limit until a retention rule is configured; the commonly mentioned 24-month policy is not a universal expiry for small accounts. No shorter LifeRPG period is claimed. See Brevo retention settings.
  • Support and moderation correspondence remains in the support Gmail mailbox until separately removed. No fixed automatic retention period has been adopted. Ask for deletion of the correspondence as part of a privacy request; this is separate from the app's database transaction. The receiving user's own email copies are outside LifeRPG's control.
  • GitHub, CDN and map logs follow their providers' policies. OpenFreeMap's published limits are described above; no fixed LifeRPG-controlled expiry is asserted for the other providers.

These are disclosed residual records, not a reason to retain an active deleted account or ordinary cloud game progress. A specific legal preservation requirement, if one applies to a request, must be explained with its scope and applicable duration rather than assumed as a blanket exception.

Confirmed in-app deletion clears that account's session, cloud caches, pending sync and temporary export. It deliberately keeps local tasks, XP, LifeCoins, Academy history, routes, steps and inventory on that device. That local database is blocked from linking its retained progress to another cloud account. Use “More → Delete all local data” separately to erase it. A web or email request cannot remotely erase an offline device or exported copies. See the deletion page for consequences and interrupted requests.

Your choices, rights and security

You can use local features without an account, manage the separate sync choices, edit your public profile, block or report players, revoke permissions, export local data and request account deletion. Depending on your location, you may also have rights of access, correction, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent or complaint to a supervisory authority. Contact liferpg.official.help@gmail.com; we may need to verify account ownership before acting. Never send a password, session token or recovery code to support.

Known application network connections use HTTPS/WSS. Server access rules separate private user records and restrict moderation operations. These measures do not guarantee absolute security, end-to-end encryption or encryption of the local database. Loss of a device or an exported file can expose information.

Age eligibility and policy changes

LifeRPG is intended for users aged 13 and older; not directed to children under 13. A higher local minimum age or parental authorisation may apply. Distribution is intended to be international, subject to country-specific requirements. Use LifeRPG only if you meet these conditions and any applicable requirements for consent. If you believe a child's information was provided contrary to these conditions, contact liferpg.official.help@gmail.com so the situation can be reviewed. This policy does not claim that age verification or parental-consent tooling is implemented.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised date. Material changes to how optional sensitive features work will also need appropriate notice and, where required, a new choice in the app.
