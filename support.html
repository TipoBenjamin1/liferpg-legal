LifeRPG is operated by Polejaev Vladislav, an individual based in the Republic of Moldova. User support is handled by the developer on behalf of the operator. Use the contacts below for help with the app or your data. Support is not a live emergency service; no 24/7 availability or fixed response time is promised.

Account issues

Email liferpg.official.help@gmail.com about sign-in, account access or sync problems. For a forgotten password, try the recovery option on the sign-in screen. Tell us your app version, device/OS and the error you see. Do not send your password, recovery codes or session tokens.

Account deletion and privacy

Email liferpg.official.help@gmail.com with the subject “LifeRPG account deletion” to request deletion without the app. Preferably write from your registered email address. If that address is unavailable, explain the access problem so account ownership can be checked. Do not send identity documents in the first message.

Read the account deletion page for the steps, consequences, local-data choices and retention details. For other access, correction or privacy requests, use the same privacy address. A request is not confirmed complete until the outcome has been checked and communicated.

Moderation, reports and abuse

Use the in-app report action on a player profile or guild, and block the player if needed. For follow-up, an abuse report or a request to review a moderation decision, email liferpg.official.help@gmail.com. Include the relevant profile/guild identifier, approximate time and a short description. Avoid repeating unnecessary sensitive information. Do not send account credentials.

Technical problems

Email liferpg.official.help@gmail.com with the app version, device/OS, steps to reproduce and expected result. Screenshots are optional; remove emails, private tasks, route coordinates and other personal information first. Do not attach the full database or JSON export unless a specific, secure diagnostic procedure has been agreed.

Keep a copy of important local information before taking troubleshooting steps. Do not uninstall LifeRPG or reset local data just to fix a sign-in issue; doing so can remove local progress that is not fully backed up in the cloud.

Privacy Policy: read the policy. Account deletion: request and instructions.
