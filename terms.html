About LifeRPG

LifeRPG is operated by Polejaev Vladislav, an individual based in the Republic of Moldova. The developer provides technical support on the operator's behalf. Contact liferpg.official.help@gmail.com for support, privacy, deletion or moderation questions.

These terms describe the use of LifeRPG's task planning, learning, RPG progress, activity and optional online features. Read the Privacy Policy and Community Rules as well. The app requires explicit acceptance of terms and community rules version 2026-11-15 before publishing or changing protected public profile or guild content. This English page explains those rules; it does not change the version recorded by the existing app.

Who may use the app

LifeRPG is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. A higher minimum age or parental authorisation may be required in your country. Use the app only where you meet the applicable requirements. These age conditions are not a promise of an implemented age-verification or parental-consent system.

Accounts and local use

Many functions work on your device without an account. An account is needed for online social features and cloud progress. Use an email address you control, protect your password and do not share your session or recovery codes. Account confirmation and recovery emails are sent through Supabase and Brevo as explained in the Privacy Policy.

You choose whether to enable the separate personal-sync and cloud-RPG features. Cloud sync is not a complete backup of every local record. An exported JSON file is not a guarantee that the app can import it or restore all progress. Keep your device and exported files secure.

Your content and other players

Only post information you are entitled to share. You remain responsible for your task descriptions, answers and public text. Public profile and guild information is visible to other players according to the feature and your settings. Do not include secrets or other people's personal information.

The Community Rules apply to usernames, names, symbolic avatars, shared skills and achievements, and guild names, descriptions and emblems. Publishing a guild or profile does not give you ownership of other players' accounts or shared guild membership. Report inappropriate content or block a player using the app's controls.

Activity, learning and game rewards

LifeRPG is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment or medical advice. Location, steps and walking validation depend on permissions, the device, data sources and surroundings. Their accuracy and uninterrupted operation are not guaranteed. Do not rely on the app for emergency assistance or safety-critical navigation.

XP and LifeCoins are virtual game units. They are not money and cannot be withdrawn as cash. Offline and cloud balances are separate. Server-validated rewards depend on the app's rules; manipulating activity, reward requests or competitive results is prohibited. Learning content and results do not constitute a professional qualification or certification.

Moderation and access restrictions

The operator can review reports, remove inappropriate public fields and restrict further publication to enforce the Community Rules. A decision can be questioned through liferpg.official.help@gmail.com. Describe the issue and the relevant profile, guild or report without sending passwords or unnecessary personal information. No fixed response time or continuous moderation coverage is promised.

Ending use and deleting data

You can stop using the app and request permanent account deletion. Signing out, disabling sync or uninstalling does not itself delete the server account. Follow Account Deletion for the in-app action and the email route that works without reinstalling.

Account deletion and local-data deletion are separate choices. Deleting an account preserves the local progress described on the deletion page and prevents that retained local database from being linked to another cloud account. Shared guild content and the disclosed technical residuals are handled as described in the Privacy Policy.
